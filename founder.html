Founder Description

Hasham Ahmed founded the Brampton Future Business Leaders Club with a commitment to democratizing access to premier business education, mentorship, and high-impact competition opportunities for ambitious youth across Canada.

Under his visionary guidance, FBLC has grown into a structured organization empowering hundreds of members, spearheading national-level events, and establishing rigorous curriculum pathways in Academics, Governance, and Technology.
